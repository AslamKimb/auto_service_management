The operating idea

One visit. One Repair Job. One complete story.

The Repair Job is the workshop’s operational spine. It connects the customer and vehicle to people, evidence, services, ERPNext transactions, release controls, and final history.

The app controls the journeyStages, responsibilities, evidence, exceptions, and release gates.
ERPNext controls the booksProjects, stock, prices, taxes, invoices, payments, credit, and ledgers.
Services hold the workEach saved service contains Parts, Labour, and Consumables—without a user-facing status.
Evidence follows the vehicleIntake, authorization, QC, road-test rows, Gate Pass, and Service History stay linked.

The automatic lifecycle

The Repair Job journey.

Select a stage to see who owns it, what must already be true, and what moves the vehicle forward. Users perform the work; the server derives the business status.

Reception opens the visit

Draft

Keys received

Responsible

Service Advisor

Required records

Customer and Customer Vehicle

Do this

  • Search registration or VIN first.
  • Capture complaint, odometer, visit reason, fuel, dates, advisor, and bay.
  • Check in the vehicle.

Output

A numbered Repair Job and one linked ERPNext Project.

Common blocker

Missing customer, vehicle, odometer, or customer concern.

Next signal

Check-in creates the Project once and moves the job into Assessment.

Understand the vehicle

Assessment

Evidence building

Responsible

Service Advisor + Workshop Technician

Records

Optional Walkaround Inspection; Diagnosis Report when diagnosis is needed; Repair Job Services.

Do this

  • Record intake condition and damage.
  • Diagnose the complaint after check-in.
  • Create saved services with Parts, Labour, and Consumables.
  • Prepare the complete commercial scope.

Output

A complete, priced service scope ready for customer approval—or diagnosis-only billing.

Common blocker

Informal work that exists in conversation but not in the service tables.

Branch

A submitted diagnosis-only visit can move directly to Billing.

Lock the agreed scope

Awaiting Approval

Customer gate

Responsible

Service Advisor; Workshop Manager for approved exceptions.

Required record

A current Customer Authorization covering the full active scope, or an approved authorization override.

Do this

  • Explain findings and estimate.
  • Record method, amount, evidence, and notes.
  • Submit the authorization.

Output

Authorized scope that workshop execution may begin.

Common blocker

Partial approval, expired evidence, or a scope revision after approval.

Important

A changed scope invalidates stale authorization and returns the job here.

Execute the approved work

In Repair

Workshop active

Responsible

Technician, Parts Interpreter, Service Advisor

Records

Repair Job Services, Project Tasks, Timesheets, Material Requests, Stock Entries.

Do this

  • Assign work by bay and technician.
  • Request and issue Parts/Consumables.
  • Record actual labour time.
  • Mark services completed.

Output

Completed, traceable work ready for independent QC.

Common blocker

Missing labour service Item, missing bay warehouse, parts shortage, or incomplete Tasks.

Rule

ERPNext owns stock movement; the app never invents ledger effects.

Prove the repair

Quality Check

Quality gate

Responsible

Workshop Manager

Required record

Submitted Quality Check; at least one Task or an approved exception.

Do this

  • Verify completion, fitment, fluids, warning lights, and cleanliness.
  • Add road-test evidence inside QC when required.
  • Pass, fail, or require rework.

Output

Passed QC moves the job toward Billing.

Common blocker

Required road test has no route, odometer, observation, or outcome.

Rework

Failed QC sends the job back to In Repair with evidence intact.

Cover every billable component

Billing

Financial gate

Responsible

Cashier + Service Advisor

Records

One or more submitted Sales Invoices and submitted payment records, or approved credit release.

Do this

  • Select all billable Parts, Labour, and Consumables.
  • Submit invoices.
  • Receive payment or validate credit policy.

Output

Complete invoice coverage and financial clearance.

Common blocker

Draft invoice, omitted component, outstanding amount beyond tolerance, or breached credit limit.

Rule

Invoice creation and payment sync do not silently move the business status.

Control physical exit

Ready for Release

Release gate

Responsible

Service Advisor + Security Gate Officer

Required record

Issued Gate Pass linked to the job, vehicle, and qualifying invoice.

Do this

  • Revalidate invoice/payment coverage.
  • Record recipient and identity reference.
  • Issue the pass.
  • Security marks it used at physical exit.

Output

A controlled, timestamped vehicle release.

Common blocker

Unsubmitted invoice, incomplete component coverage, or unresolved payment/credit.

Rule

An invoice is not permission for the vehicle to leave. The Gate Pass is.

Finish the record once

Closed

Visit complete

Responsible

Service Advisor / authorized closing user

Required evidence

Gate Pass used and release controls satisfied.

System action

  • Closes the Repair Job.
  • Creates Service History exactly once.
  • Updates the vehicle’s odometer/service snapshot.

Output

A searchable, auditable visit history for the vehicle and customer.

Common blocker

Gate Pass issued but not used.

Terminal rule

Closed and Cancelled jobs cannot be reopened through ordinary workflow actions.

Cancellation lane: a validated active job may be cancelled with a reason before closure. Cancellation stops the workflow; it does not erase stock, invoices, logs, or other downstream facts.

Practical branch paths

Seven playbooks for real work.

The happy path is only the beginning. Open a playbook when the visit takes a normal workshop turn.

Normal repair
DraftAssessmentApprovalRepairQCBillingReleaseClosed

Capture the vehicle, create the scope, secure approval, execute and record work, pass QC, invoice every billable component, clear payment, use the Gate Pass, and close.

Diagnosis-only visit
AssessmentBillingReleaseClosed

Submit the Diagnosis Report, bill only completed diagnostic work, complete release controls, then close. No repair authorization or repair QC is invented.

QC failure and rework
Quality CheckIn RepairQuality Check

Record the failed observation and required correction. Return the job to repair, complete the rework, then submit fresh QC evidence. Do not edit a failure into a pass.

Partial or changed authorization
Awaiting ApprovalScope changeAwaiting Approval

Authorization must cover the full current scope. If the scope changes, the revision makes stale approval invalid. Record a new authorization before repair continues.

Corporate credit release
BillingCredit checksReady for Release

Use configured payment terms, respect ERPNext credit limits, and record the approved release basis. An override is manager-controlled and evidence-backed.

Fleet campaign
CampaignSeparate Repair JobsIndividual closure

The campaign groups visits for one corporate customer. Each vehicle still receives its own Repair Job, invoices, Gate Pass, and Service History.

Cancellation
Active stageValidated reasonCancelled

Cancel before terminal completion with a clear reason. Reconcile any ERPNext stock or commercial documents normally; cancellation is not a data eraser.

Non-negotiable controls

Never bypass these.

No illegal jumps

Use the app’s actions and evidence. Do not force `job_status` or use database writes to mimic a workflow transition.

No unauthorized work

Repair requires current full-scope authorization or a properly approved, evidence-backed override.

No missing invoice lines

Every billable Part, Labour, and Consumable must be covered by a submitted invoice before release.

No invoice-only release

A vehicle needs financial clearance and an issued Gate Pass. Security records actual exit by using the pass.

No duplicate history

Service History is created automatically and idempotently when the job closes. Do not recreate it manually.

No casual overrides

Only Workshop Manager or Auto Service Admin may approve controlled exceptions, with reason and evidence.

Plain-language reference

Glossary.

Repair Job
The central record for one vehicle visit and its complete workshop story.
Repair Job Service
A saved unit of workshop work containing Parts, Labour, and Consumables. It has no user-facing eligibility status.
Customer Authorization
Submitted evidence that the customer approved the full current service scope and amount.
Quality Check
The manager’s verification record. Required road-test evidence is stored as child rows inside it.
Gate Pass
The controlled release document. “Issued” permits collection; “Used” records physical exit.
Invoice coverage
Every billable component is linked to a submitted Sales Invoice; a draft reservation is not enough.
Credit release
A controlled unpaid release allowed only by configured terms, available credit, and required approval evidence.
Service History
The single closure snapshot automatically created for a Repair Job and linked to its vehicle.
Repair Job Override
A submitted, evidence-backed exception that only Workshop Manager or Auto Service Admin can approve.
ERPNext authority
ERPNext—not the workshop app—is authoritative for Projects, stock, prices, taxes, invoices, payments, credit, and ledgers.
